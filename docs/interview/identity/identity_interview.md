# Identitty Interview

1. Open-Ended Discovery (Broad Identity & Self-Perception)

    Open the floor on their shopping persona:

        "If you had to describe what kind of online shopper you are to someone who has never seen you buy anything, how would you describe yourself?"

        Follow-up: "What habits or tendencies make you say that?"

    Explore their relationship with shopping and digital routines:

        "How does browsing or buying on e-commerce platforms fit into your day-to-day life?"

        Probe: "Is it purposeful and planned, or more of a background habit when you have downtime?"

    Understand their financial and value mindset:

        "How do you view the trade-off between spending time to save money versus spending money to save time when buying online?"

        Follow-up: "Where do you draw the line where comparing prices is no longer worth your effort?"

    Social and household shopping role:

        "Among your friends, colleagues, or family, what reputation do you have when it comes to online shopping or finding good prices?"

        Probe: "Are you the person people ask to hunt down a deal or check a voucher, or do you prefer to keep your shopping to yourself?"

2. Semi-Structured Probes (Behavioral Anchors)

    Tech comfort with external tools:

        "When you use platforms like Shopee, do you stick strictly to the official app/site, or do you use browser extensions, Telegram bots, or third-party tools to help with tasks?"

        Follow-up: "How comfortable are you trying out a new digital tool just to optimize your shopping?"

    Primary device identity:

        "Between your smartphone and a PC/laptop, which device captures the majority of your online shopping time, and why that specific device?"

3. Structured Checkpoints (Demographic & Classification Data)

Use this section to explicitly categorize their profile against standard persona metrics.

    Occupational and living setup:

        "To help categorize your background, what is your current profession or main field of work/study?"

        "What does your current living situation look like (living alone, with family, or with housemates)?"

    Income and discretionary spending range:

        "Roughly what bracket does your personal monthly income or allowance fall into?"

            Under 5 million VND

            5 to 10 million VND

            10 to 20 million VND

            Over 20 million VND

        "Roughly how much of that is typically spent on personal online orders per month?"

    Archetype forced-choice:

        "Out of these four archetypes, which one feels closest to your core shopping identity?"

            The Deal Hunter: Driven by the satisfaction of finding the lowest possible number.

            The Fast & Convenient Buyer: Willing to pay a bit more to avoid spending extra minutes searching.

            The Cautious Researcher: Takes days to verify reviews, shops, and price history before committing.

            The Impulse/Mood Buyer: Buys primarily based on discovery, sales banners, and momentary interest.
